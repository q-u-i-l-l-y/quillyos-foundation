## STATEMENT

To build an open, ethical, modular ecosystem that bridges traditional and advanced
systems — equipping individuals with personal compute nodes that augment human
capability across health, research, communication, and creative domains — while
ensuring that every technological generation enhances rather than replaces what
came before.

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

1. **Revenue Autonomy** — The ecosystem generates sufficient revenue to fund its own
   research and development without external dependency.

2. **Knowledge Immortality** — No knowledge is lost. Every discovery, failure, and
   insight is preserved in the canonical memory with full context.

3. **Protocol Survival** — The Nexus Protocol outlives any individual implementation,
   model, or hardware generation.

4. **Health Empowerment** — Individuals can observe, correlate, and understand their
   own health signals through personal compute nodes.

5. **Supply Chain Intelligence** — The ecosystem can map, predict, and optimize resource
   trajectories across traditional and advanced supply networks.

6. **Cross-Disciplinary Bridge** — Medicine, materials science, quantum research, and
   information systems flow through a unified protocol.

7. **Ethical Immutability** — The ethical framework is never compromised for short-term
   gain. Every decision is reviewed against the principles in ETHICS.md.

## ANTI-GOALS

What we will NOT do:
- Replace human judgment with automated decisions
- Create dependency on proprietary systems
- Optimize for engagement over substance
- Treat users as data sources rather than stewards
- Compromise privacy for convenience
- Accept planned obsolescence as inevitable
