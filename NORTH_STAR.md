## THE DESTINATION

A world where every individual carries a personal compute node that:
- Monitors their health as a continuous, probabilistic signal
- Connects them to a distributed mesh of knowledge and capability
- Augments their creativity, research, and communication
- Remains repairable, upgradable, and compatible across generations
- Operates under their full control, with their data remaining theirs

This is not a product. This is a **capability**.
