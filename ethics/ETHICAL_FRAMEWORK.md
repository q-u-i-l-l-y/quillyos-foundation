# ETHICAL FRAMEWORK
## QuillyOS Foundational Ethics v1.0

---

## CORE ETHICAL PRINCIPLES

The architecture optimizes for:

### 1. HUMAN AGENCY
Humans direct. Machines assist. No automated system may override the human steward's ethical boundaries or strategic intent.

- Layer 0 remains human.
- Agents propose. Humans decide.
- Exit is always possible.
- The system serves the human, not the reverse.

### 2. MODULARITY
Any component should be replaceable without redesigning the whole. This applies to ethics as well as technology.

- Ethical frameworks can evolve.
- No single ethical module is absolute.
- Changes require evidence and consensus.
- Modularity prevents ethical lock-in.

### 3. REPAIRABILITY
Systems can be fixed, not just replaced. This includes ethical failures.

- Mistakes are documented.
- Corrections are versioned.
- Accountability is traceable.
- No "black box" ethical decisions.

### 4. TRANSPARENCY
Decisions are explainable and auditable. Every ethical choice has a rationale.

- Decision records include ethical considerations.
- Agents document their reasoning.
- Contradictions are flagged.
- Nothing is hidden by design.

### 5. PRIVACY
Data sovereignty remains with the individual. The system does not extract; it serves.

- Personal data stays local by default.
- Sharing requires explicit consent.
- Mesh nodes do not surveil.
- Health data is encrypted and user-controlled.

### 6. INTEROPERABILITY
Systems work together through open standards. No vendor lock-in, no platform capture.

- Protocols are open.
- Interfaces are documented.
- Exit is frictionless.
- Data is portable.

### 7. SCIENTIFIC INTEGRITY
Claims require evidence. Hype is not a substitute for rigor.

- No capability claimed without validation.
- No technology adopted before evidence.
- Metamaterials and quantum are explored, not assumed.
- Research is evidence-based.

### 8. CONTINUOUS LEARNING
The system improves through feedback. Ethical frameworks evolve with understanding.

- Regular ethical review.
- New evidence updates frameworks.
- Past decisions are revisited.
- The framework is a living document.

## SPECIFIC COMMITMENTS

### Health & Medical
- The ENDS device and health platform augment, not replace, medical professionals.
- Health data is user-owned and encrypted.
- Diagnoses are suggestions, not prescriptions.
- Safety testing precedes deployment.

### AI & Automation
- AI agents do not make irreversible decisions without human review.
- Autonomous systems have kill switches.
- Agent reasoning is documented and inspectable.
- No agent may modify its own ethical constraints.

### Revenue & Markets
- Revenue sustains research; it does not exploit.
- Arbitrage is information-based, not predatory.
- Supply chain positioning supports life, not extraction.
- Financial experiments are documented and reversible.

### Hardware & Environment
- Devices are repairable and upgradable.
- Materials are sourced responsibly where possible.
- Energy consumption is minimized.
- End-of-life is planned, not ignored.

### Mesh & Network
- Nodes join voluntarily.
- Nodes leave freely.
- Reputation is transparent.
- No node surveils another without consent.

## ETHICAL DECISION PROCESS

When an ethical question arises:

1. **Identify stakeholders** — Who is affected?
2. **Gather evidence** — What do we know?
3. **Apply principles** — Which ethical principles apply?
4. **Identify conflicts** — Do principles conflict?
5. **Propose resolution** — What is the recommended path?
6. **Human review** — Does the steward approve?
7. **Record decision** — Document in `decision_records/`
8. **Monitor outcomes** — Did the decision produce expected results?

---

## VIOLATION PROTOCOL

If an agent or system violates ethical principles:

1. **Halt** — Stop the violating behavior immediately.
2. **Document** — Record what happened and why.
3. **Assess** — Determine scope and impact.
4. **Correct** — Fix the issue and prevent recurrence.
5. **Review** — Governance Steward reviews and records decision.
6. **Communicate** — Relevant parties are informed.
