# SECTION 1 — THE NORTH STAR

Revenue generation is not the destination.

Revenue is fuel.

The long-term objective is the development of an open, ethical, modular human–technology ecosystem centered on a handheld platform that evolves into a distributed network of intelligent health, sensing, communication, and research nodes.

The initial physical platform is an ENDS-derived modular handheld device.

The platform is not viewed as a consumer product.

It is viewed as an evolving systems interface.

Eventually the device becomes:

• sensing platform

• health monitoring platform

• computation node

• communications node

• research platform

• modular expansion platform

• edge AI platform

• distributed mesh participant

The hardware should evolve alongside software rather than becoming obsolete.
