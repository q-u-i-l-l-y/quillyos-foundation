# REFERENCES
## External Influences and Prior Art

## SYSTEMS & ARCHITECTURES

### Git as Constitution
The concept of using Git not merely as version control but as the canonical memory and governance layer of a distributed system.

### Event-Driven Architecture
Loosely coupled systems communicating through events rather than direct calls. Enables scalability and agent independence.

### Mesh Networking
Distributed network topology where nodes connect directly to each other rather than through a central hub. Resilient and scalable.

### Knowledge Graphs
Structured representations of entities and relationships. Foundation for semantic search, reasoning, and context management.

### Semantic Versioning
Versioning scheme (major.minor.patch) for APIs, skills, and protocols. Ensures compatibility and clear change communication.

---

## PHILOSOPHICAL INFLUENCES

### Continuous Augmentation
The principle that systems should evolve by enhancing rather than replacing previous generations. Rejects planned obsolescence.

### Human-in-the-Loop
AI systems designed to augment rather than replace human decision-making. Human agency remains central.

### Open Systems Architecture
Systems designed for interoperability, modularity, and extensibility. No vendor lock-in. Exit is always possible.

### Ethical Technology
Technology designed with explicit ethical constraints: privacy, transparency, human agency, scientific integrity.

---

## TECHNOLOGY DOMAINS

### ENDS (Electronic Nicotine Delivery Systems)
The initial hardware platform. Modular, handheld, capable of sensor integration and health monitoring.

### Metamaterials
Artificial materials with properties not found in nature. Potential applications in sensing, signal manipulation, and energy interaction.

### Quantum Communication
Emerging communication technologies leveraging quantum mechanics. Explored as future interfaces, not assumed capabilities.

### Edge AI
AI inference on local, low-power devices. Essential for privacy-preserving, low-latency applications.

### Federated Learning
Machine learning approach where models are trained across decentralized devices without centralizing data. Privacy-preserving.

## TOOLS & PLATFORMS

### n8n
Open-source workflow automation tool. The execution layer for QuillyOS workflows.

### GitHub
Version control and collaboration platform. The canonical memory layer for QuillyOS.

### Termux
Linux environment for Android. The primary deployment platform for PicoClaw.

### Raspberry Pi
Low-cost single-board computers. The compute layer for mesh nodes and cluster operations.

### Markdown / YAML
Plain-text formats for structured documentation. The primary data formats for the Nexus Protocol.

## RESEARCH AREAS

### Digital Health
Technology-mediated health monitoring, diagnostics, and intervention. The primary application domain for the ENDS platform.

### Sensor Networks
Distributed collections of sensors that collect and share data. Foundation for environmental and health monitoring.

### Biotech Interfaces
Technologies that bridge biological and synthetic systems. Future direction for the ENDS platform.

### Supply Chain Ethics
Positioning supply chains to support life and lifestyle rather than extract value. Core to the revenue philosophy.
