## THE DECISION LADDER

Every decision passes through seven rungs:

```
1. OBSERVE — What is happening?
2. EVIDENCE — What do we know?
3. CONTEXT — What is the situation?
4. PRINCIPLES — What does ETHICS.md say?
5. OPTIONS — What could we do?
6. IMPACT — What would each option do?
7. CHOOSE — What will we do?
```

No decision skips a rung. No decision is made in haste.
