## GRAMMAR

Nexus statements follow the structure:

```
[OBSERVER] observes [EVIDENCE] in [CONTEXT] leading to [INFERENCE] with [CONFIDENCE]
```

Example:
```
Kimi observes increased biometrics variance in sleep_context leading to
possible_sleep_disruption with confidence_0.72
```

This structure is machine-parseable, human-readable, and model-agnostic.
