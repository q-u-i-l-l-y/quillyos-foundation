## PHILOSOPHY

Revenue is fuel. It is not the destination.

The revenue model is an **adaptive portfolio** — not a fixed plan.
It is designed to be:
- **Strategic** — aligned with long-term capabilities
- **Non-predatory** — value creation over extraction
- **Diversified** — no single point of failure
- **Automated** — minimal human intervention for routine operations
- **Transparent** — all revenue flows are documented in the knowledge base
- **Evolving** — priorities reassessed through autonomous agent research

## NON-PREDATORY PRINCIPLES

1. **No dark patterns** — revenue comes from genuine value, not manipulation
2. **No data exploitation** — user data is never sold or shared without explicit consent
3. **No lock-in** — customers can leave at any time with their data
4. **Fair pricing** — prices reflect value, not extraction potential
5. **Inclusive access** — revenue models include free tiers for those who need them
